#+TITLE: Opt-in numerical bread rank policy (issue 219)
#+OPTIONS: toc:2

* Status and boundary

Design adopted for the opt-in implementation, 2026-10-23.
This reconciles [[https://github.com/ligon/Emu-GMM/issues/219][issue 219]] with the tested machinery in [[file:../.coder/ledger.org][the prior-art ledger]], Section 11.
A small eigenvalue of the bread at a fitted point indicates numerical weakness, not population nonidentification.
The policy below computes a retained-subspace approximation to the asymptotic law; it makes no boundary-valid inference or coverage claim.
The existing count-only result remains the default, including its bitwise =inv= path when the nominal gauge count is zero (=src/emu_gmm/_internal/pinv_eigvalrule.py:116=).

* Construction and coordinates

Proposed public entry points:

#+begin_src python
law = result.asymptotic(rank_policy=NumericalRankPolicy(relative_tol=1e-10))
# Equivalently: AsymptoticLaw(result, rank_policy=NumericalRankPolicy(...))
report = law.rank_report
cov = law.cov()                         # retained-subspace covariance
se = law.leaf("phi").se()               # same policy through a leaf view
sensitivity = law.functional_sensitivity(f)
#+end_src

=rank_policy=None= retains the present count-only path, with no eigenspectrum scan added to scalar fits.
=rank_report= is then =None= and =functional_sensitivity= requires an opted-in policy.
=NumericalRankPolicy= accepts a finite =0 < relative_tol < 1= and records =coordinates="native_ambient_flat"= and =metric="euclidean_frobenius"=; other conventions require a later design.
The flat order is the component/leaf order of =ManifoldSpec=, with row-major entries within each leaf (=src/emu_gmm/manifolds/spec.py:23= and =src/emu_gmm/inference/functional_se.py:161=).
The metric is the Euclidean norm on that vector, matching the embedded Frobenius metric on =PSDFixedRank= (=src/emu_gmm/manifolds/psd_fixed_rank.py:152=).
It does not use the point-dependent affine-invariant =Positive= or pullback =Interval= manifold metrics (=src/emu_gmm/manifolds/positive.py:82=, =src/emu_gmm/manifolds/interval.py:115=).
A relative cutoff is parameter-scale dependent: changing units or rescaling a leaf can change the retained rank.
The report therefore exposes coordinate labels, leaf shapes, metric, and tolerance rather than describing rank as invariant.

* Spectral rule and invalid bread

Reuse the law's realised weighting-aware ingredients \(B=G'\Lambda G\) and \(M=G'\Lambda V\Lambda G\), where \(V\) is raw meat and \(\Lambda\) is the frozen realised weight (=src/emu_gmm/_internal/asymptotic.py:35=).
The opted-in branch belongs in that shared internal assembly and returns both covariance and rank report; the existing count-only call keeps its matrix return and exact computation.
Symmetrise \(B\), then obtain its signed ascending eigenspectrum \(\lambda_i\) and orthonormal eigenvectors \(q_i\).
Index the ascending eigenvalues from \(i=0\) to \(D-1\), let \(g\) be the nominal gauge count, and set \(\lambda_{\max}=\lambda_{D-1}\) and \(c=t\lambda_{\max}\) for =relative_tol= \(t\).
For a valid positive top eigenvalue, exclude every \(i<g\) by count and every \(\lambda_i\leq c\) by tolerance; retain the others.
This uses the union of the mandatory count and cutoff, so it can drop weak positive directions beyond \(g\) without changing the default rule.
Set \(P_E=\sum_{i\in E}q_iq_i'\) and \(B_t^+=\sum_{i\notin E}q_iq_i'/\lambda_i\), then report \(\Sigma_t=B_t^+MB_t^+\), symmetrised.
The effective numerical rank is \(D-|E|\); =extra_excluded= is \(|E|-g\), a count only, not a classification of particular eigenvectors as nongauge.
Here rank deficiency belongs to the ambient bread \(B\), whereas \(g\) is the nominal dimension of a geometric gauge orbit on the regular manifold stratum; neither number identifies the other, especially at a singular factor.

The =rank_report= is an immutable readout containing the selected policy, signed \(\lambda_i\), \(c\), sorted retained mask, \(P_E\), effective rank, \(g\), =extra_excluded=, largest excluded and smallest retained eigenvalues, and both distances from the cutoff.
Store the whole projector, not an ordered subset of eigenvectors: a basis within a degenerate nullspace is arbitrary.
If the retained/excluded spectral gap is at machine precision, mark =projector_boundary_ambiguous=; rotation invariance of that particular split is not asserted.
Record the numerical negative-eigenvalue allowance \(\delta=64D\epsilon_{64}\lambda_{\max}\), separately from the user cutoff.
An identically zero spectrum has status =all_zero_bread=; any other \(\lambda_{\max}\leq0\) has status =no_positive_bread=.
When \(\lambda_{\max}>0\) and \(\lambda_{\min}<-\delta\), the status is =materially_negative_bread=.
Any invalid status yields NaN covariance and SEs paired with that named status; =sample()= and =quantile()= refuse rather than presenting a degenerate zero-variance law.
For invalid bread, retain the signed spectrum and diagnostic projector (the full identity when no positive direction exists), but do not invert it for inference.
Tiny negative eigenvalues within \(\delta\) remain visible in the signed spectrum and are excluded by the positive cutoff.
This invalid-bread rule is opt-in only; it does not change count-only behavior.

* Covariance, functionals, and geometry

On a valid opted-in law, =cov()=, =se()=, =coef_table=, =functional_se(f)=, named leaf queries, =quantile()=, and =sample()= all use \(\Sigma_t\).
The numbers are conditional on fixing the excluded ambient coordinates in the numerical linearization; =sample()= draws the corresponding degenerate Gaussian.
They are not sampling uncertainty for those excluded coordinates and do not certify an identified quotient law.
The report labels this interpretation as =retained_subspace=; the sensitivity query below is separate and cannot silently rewrite an SE as zero or NaN.
=functional_se(f)= may be finite even when \(J_fP_E\) or \(H_fP_E\) is nonzero: it is a conditional retained-subspace readout, and callers must query =functional_sensitivity(f)= before interpreting it as uncertainty for =f=.
An excluded raw coordinate can have a zero conditional SE, so an opted-in =coef_table= must label the policy and excluded-coordinate loading and suppress its t-statistic and p-value where loading is nonzero.
=J_pvalue=, =J_pvalue_adjusted=, =n_overid=, identification diagnostics, optimization, and the empirical-law grade stay unchanged.

For a scalar or one-dimensional twice JAX-differentiable =f(components)=, =functional_sensitivity(f)= returns per-output first-order \(\|J_fP_E\|_2\) and second-order \(\|H_fP_E\|_F\), plus the projected arrays and derivative orders checked.
Here \(H_f\) is the full ambient Hessian at the fitted point, so \(H_fP_E\) includes mixed retained/excluded curvature; \(P_EH_fP_E\) alone would miss it.
No finite-perturbation radius or Boolean "invariant" certificate is inferred from these derivatives.
At \(A=0\), =f(A)=vech(AA')= has \(J_f=0\) but nonzero \(H_fP_E\), so the report exposes the zero-gradient counterexample.
A cubic functional at zero can have both reported norms equal to zero; the report states that higher orders remain unassessed.
The raw derivative norms use the declared ambient metric and have the units of their respective derivatives.
Nondifferentiable functionals, including an individual eigenvalue at a repeated eigenvalue, cannot acquire a valid sensitivity report from this API.

The excluded projector contains nominal gauge and numerically weak directions together.
The first slice reports sensitivity to this *whole* excluded subspace and does not label a particular eigenvector "gauge" or report a nongauge-only score.
A future nongauge decomposition must construct geometric orbit directions \(A\Omega\) for skew \(\Omega\) under the declared metric, then test their inclusion in \(P_E\); sorting eigenvectors is insufficient.
For each =PSDFixedRank(n,k)= leaf, persist singular values of the fitted factor and a =geometry_status=.
The first-slice machine rule calls it singular when \(s_{\max}=0\) or \(s_{\min}\leq64\max(n,k)\epsilon_{64}s_{\max}\); this is a numerical geometry flag, not a population-rank test.
If the factor is singular, retain algebraic numerical readouts for other identified leaves but label the regular-quotient interpretation unavailable.
Near-rank-loss singular values remain visible even when that machine rule calls the factor full rank; no fixed scale threshold is promoted to a boundary-inference claim.

* Persistence and compatibility

Version the inert law format when policy semantics are added: new artifacts carry a typed policy/report and projector in the NPZ payload, while the new loader accepts schema-1 artifacts as count-only.
An older loader must refuse the new schema rather than load a truncated covariance without its interpretation.
Persist the policy, coordinate/metric convention, leaf layout, signed spectrum, cutoff, mask, effective rank, nominal gauge count, extra count, margins, negative allowance, geometry status/singular values, \(P_E\), and assembled \(\Sigma_t\).
Validate dimensions, finite policy scalars, mask count, projector symmetry/idempotence, and covariance shape on load.
Copy the report unchanged through live save, load, and re-save; rebuild sensitivity from persisted components and \(P_E\) with the same ambient derivative rule.
Loaded opted-in laws retain the same conditional =cov()= / =functional_se(f)= behavior, separate sensitivity query, and coefficient-table suppression; schema-1 laws retain their original count-only query behavior.
The current format retains components and assembled covariance but omits \(G,\Lambda,V\) (=src/emu_gmm/persistence.py:281=); changing =relative_tol= after reload must therefore raise a clear error.
Post-load re-ranking is deferred until an artifact also persists the original sandwich ingredients.
=AsymptoticLaw.from_moments(components, sigma)= cannot invent a numerical-rank report from \(\Sigma\) and remains count-only.

* Validation gate for implementation

- Default fidelity :: =rank_policy=None= is bitwise identical to the current scalar and manifold paths; existing golden sandwich and count-only tests remain green (=tests/test_result_law_fidelity.py=, =tests/test_pinv_eigvalrule.py=).
- Spectral cases :: a weak positive direction below/above the cutoff, multiple exact zeros beyond \(g\), a tied nullspace or ambiguous boundary, all-zero bread, and a materially negative signed eigenvalue give the stated masks, projectors, ranks, and status.
- Gauge rotation :: for full-rank \(A\) and orthogonal \(Q\), the excluded projector and covariance transform with the ambient rotation; gauge-invariant functional SEs and sensitivity norms agree, while raw \(A\)-coordinate SEs may differ.
- Functional cases :: a retained-only \(\phi\), an excluded-linear functional, =vech(AA')= at exact zero, a mixed-curvature quadratic, and a cubic at zero distinguish SEs from derivative sensitivity; the opted-in coefficient table flags excluded loading and suppresses its t/p columns.
- Persistence :: live/load/re-save preserves covariance, policy, projector, labels, leaf queries, sensitivity, and J quantities; a schema-1 fixture defaults to count-only; a tampered projector is rejected; retuning a loaded law refuses.
- Compatibility :: no change to =OptimizationResult='s criterion, =n_overid=, J tests, =compute_cond_info=, or the empirical-law grade; =make quick-check= and =make check= are the implementation gates.

No Monte Carlo coverage target follows from this numerical-policy slice.
Boundary-valid inference and its calibration require a separate specification.
