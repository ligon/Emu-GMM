#+TITLE: Horizontal Information Under MCAR for Fixed-Rank PSD Parameters
#+SUBTITLE: A companion to docs/mcar-asymptotics.org
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:t todo:nil

* Scope

The Euclidean MCAR note establishes a positive-definite limit covariance under its stated covariance assumptions, then separately assumes a full-column-rank moment Jacobian for asymptotic normality.
The same division is needed for a fixed-rank PSD parameter: overlap concerns the covariance of observed moments, whereas identification concerns how those moments change along the quotient manifold.
Neither property supplies the other.

We give a local statement about information at a full-column-rank factor \(A_0\), not a general consistency or central limit theorem on manifolds.
The result also applies to a product with Euclidean leaves by adding their tangent coordinates to the horizontal space.
All covariance claims below use the unregularised population limit; a finite-sample ridge can make a matrix invertible without establishing identification.

* The quotient and the two assumptions

Write \(\Gamma=AA^\top\), with \(A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times k}\) of column rank \(k\), and identify \(A\) with \(AQ\) for \(Q\in O(k)\).
At \(A_0\), the vertical and horizontal spaces under the Frobenius metric are
\[
\mathcal{V}_{A_0}=\{A_0\Omega:\Omega^\top=-\Omega\},\qquad
\mathcal{H}_{A_0}=\{Z:A_0^\top Z=Z^\top A_0\}=\mathcal{V}_{A_0}^{\perp}.
\]
The horizontal dimension is \(q=nk-k(k-1)/2\).
For a product parameter, \(\mathcal{H}\) is the direct sum of the horizontal spaces of its fixed-rank factors and the ordinary tangent spaces of its ungauged leaves; \(q\) is the sum of their dimensions.
This matches the repository's ambient-shape storage and gauge count in =PSDFixedRank= and =ManifoldSpec= ([[file:implementation-plan-v2-manifold.org][v2 manifold plan]], Section 2.1).

Let \(m(A)=E[\psi(X,A)]\in\mathbb{R}^M\) be differentiable at \(A_0\), with \(m(A_0)=0\) and \(m(AQ)=m(A)\).
Write \(G=Dm_{A_0}:\mathbb{R}^{n\times k}\to\mathbb{R}^M\) for its derivative in ambient factor coordinates.
Gauge invariance gives \(G[A_0\Omega]=0\) for every skew-symmetric \(\Omega\); it says nothing about \(G\) on \(\mathcal{H}_{A_0}\).
For a product parameter \(\theta=(A,\phi)\), read \(G\) below as the full derivative \(Dm_{\theta_0}\), including its ungauged leaves.

- Covariance assumption (COV) :: The limiting covariance \(V^{\mathrm{lim}}\) of \(\sqrt{N}\,\bar m_X(A_0)\) exists and is positive definite.
  Under the iid MCAR and regularity conditions of [[file:mcar-asymptotics.org][the Euclidean companion]], its Theorem 2 gives \(V^{\mathrm{lim}}=R\circ V\), and its Proposition 1 supplies the sufficient condition \(R\succ0\) and \(V\succ0\).
  Positive pairwise overlap alone doesn't imply COV; the proposition's sufficient condition isn't necessary either, since complete observation has \(R=\boldsymbol{1}\boldsymbol{1}^\top\) and can still have \(V^{\mathrm{lim}}=V\succ0\).
- Quotient identification assumption (HID) :: The restriction \(G|_{\mathcal{H}_{A_0}}\) is injective, equivalently \(\operatorname{rank}(GB)=q\) for any matrix \(B\) whose columns form a basis of \(\mathcal{H}_{A_0}\) in ambient coordinates.
  In particular, \(M\geq q\) is necessary.
  HID is a local rank condition; a separate global identification condition is needed to select the intended equivalence class consistently.

* Restricted information

The covariance assumption lets us distinguish true weak identification from an ill-defined inverse weight.
Define the data information quadratic form on the horizontal space by
\[
\mathcal{I}_{\mathcal{H}}(Z_1,Z_2)
=\langle G[Z_1],(V^{\mathrm{lim}})^{-1}G[Z_2]\rangle,
\qquad Z_1,Z_2\in\mathcal{H}_{A_0}.
\]
With an orthonormal horizontal basis \(B\), its matrix is \(I_B=(GB)^\top(V^{\mathrm{lim}})^{-1}(GB)\).
Changing the orthonormal basis changes \(I_B\) by orthogonal congruence, so positive definiteness is intrinsic to the quotient point.

** Proposition (horizontal information and identification)

Under COV, \(\mathcal{I}_{\mathcal{H}}\) is positive definite if and only if HID holds.
Equivalently, the only null directions of the ambient information \(G^\top(V^{\mathrm{lim}})^{-1}G\) are the vertical gauge directions if and only if HID holds.

/Proof./ For every horizontal \(Z\),
\[
\mathcal{I}_{\mathcal{H}}(Z,Z)
=\bigl\|(V^{\mathrm{lim}})^{-1/2}G[Z]\bigr\|^2.
\]
COV makes \((V^{\mathrm{lim}})^{-1/2}\) invertible, hence this expression vanishes exactly when \(G[Z]=0\).
It is strictly positive for every nonzero horizontal \(Z\) exactly when \(G|_{\mathcal{H}_{A_0}}\) is injective.
Gauge invariance puts \(\mathcal{V}_{A_0}\) in \(\ker G\), while the full-column-rank factor gives the orthogonal decomposition \(\mathbb{R}^{n\times k}=\mathcal{V}_{A_0}\oplus\mathcal{H}_{A_0}\).
Thus the ambient kernel equals \(\mathcal{V}_{A_0}\) exactly when the horizontal kernel is trivial. \(\square\)

For an all-Euclidean parameter, the vertical space is zero and the horizontal space is the whole parameter space.
HID then reduces to the full-column-rank \(G\) condition explicitly added in Theorem 5 of [[file:mcar-asymptotics.org][the Euclidean companion]].
With the companion's separate consistency and differentiability assumptions, the inverse of \(I_B\) is the usual local efficient covariance in horizontal coordinates; this proposition alone does not prove those asymptotic claims.

* Complete overlap does not identify a quotient parameter

Take =PSDFixedRank(2,1)=, so \(A=a=(a_1,a_2)^\top\neq0\), \(\Gamma=aa^\top\), and the gauge action is the discrete sign change \(a\mapsto-a\).
There is no continuous vertical direction: \(\mathcal{H}_a=\mathbb{R}^2\) and \(q=2\).
Observe both moments for every unit, and let the iid residual vector be
\[
\psi_i(a)=
\begin{pmatrix} \|a\|^2-1 \\ 2(\|a\|^2-1) \end{pmatrix}+\varepsilon_i,
\qquad E[\varepsilon_i]=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_i)=I_2.
\]
For example, \(\varepsilon_i\) may be standard bivariate normal and independent across units.
The moment map is gauge-invariant, the observation indicators equal one, and at \(a_0=(1,0)^\top\) the moment covariance is \(V^{\mathrm{lim}}=I_2\succ0\).
Nevertheless,
\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\4&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
I_B=G^\top G=\begin{pmatrix}20&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The angular direction \((0,1)^\top\) is horizontal and unidentified.
All nearby points on the unit circle have the same zero population moment vector, although their \(aa^\top\) matrices differ.
Thus even complete overlap, COV, and gauge invariance together don't imply HID.

* Scope and diagnostic reading

The full-column-rank condition on \(A_0\) matters.
At a factor with rank below \(k\), the point lies outside =PSDFixedRank(n,k)=; the quotient's fixed-dimensional horizontal decomposition and the projection's Lyapunov solve need not apply.
The proposition also doesn't identify a particular model's quotient parameter: that requires proving HID, and global identification if a consistent estimator is claimed, for that model's moment map.

The implementation stores ambient-shape tangents and drops the known \(k(k-1)/2\) gauge eigenvalues by count for its pseudoinverse ([[file:../src/emu_gmm/_internal/pinv_eigvalrule.py][pinv rule]]).
The diagnostic =result.diagnostics.cond_info["exclude_gauge"]= applies the same gauge count and reports the condition number of the remaining spectrum ([[file:../src/emu_gmm/diagnostics.py][diagnostics]]; [[file:../tests/manifolds/test_cond_info_gauge.py][tests]]).
When no objective penalty contributes, an additional small retained eigenvalue warns of near failure of HID, as in the counterexample.
The diagnostic uses the regularised sample covariance and includes an objective penalty if present, so it is a numerical warning rather than a proof of population identification; a penalty may conceal a data-only rank defect.
When a penalty is present, =cond_info["data_only"]= removes its curvature but still uses the sample \(V^\star\) and retains the gauge eigenvalues; no member of this diagnostic trio is both data-only and gauge-excluded.
With zero gauge dimension, =exclude_gauge= aliases =raw=, so the counterexample's singularity remains visible.
