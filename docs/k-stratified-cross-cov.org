#+title: Design-matched derivative-moment cross covariance for K (#141)

* Scope and existing definitions

Automatic K inference supports pure =StratifiedCovariance= and both modes of =DesignAwareCovariance= when the measure supplies empirical contributions, masks, and weights.
The derivative/moment cross covariance uses the same TT/SS/TS dependence and normalization as the selected moment covariance, including the opt-in fixed-stratum exposure projection (#145).
Other measures can supply an explicit matched =score_cov_fn=.
The K correction in =src/emu_gmm/inference/k_statistic.py= subtracts \(\widehat\Sigma_{G_a,m}(V^\star)^{-1}m\) from Jacobian column \(G_a\).
The target \(\widehat\Sigma_{G_a,m}\) must use the same dependence structure and \(N_jN_k\) scale as the moment covariance \(V\); see =.coder/ledger.org= Sections 3, 4, and 7.6--7.7.

* Matched per-cell, per-pair form

At a fixed null parameter, the measure's contribution methods already return
\[
g_{ij}=d_{ij}w_i\psi_j(x_i,\theta_0),\qquad
D_{ija}=d_{ij}w_i\partial_a\psi_j(x_i,\theta_0).
\]
For PSU \(g\), let \(T^m_{gj}=\sum_{i\in g}g_{ij}\) and \(T^D_{gja}=\sum_{i\in g}D_{ija}\).
Let \(s_{gj}=\mathbf 1\{\sum_{i\in g}d_{ij}>0\}\), \(u_{g,jk}=s_{gj}s_{gk}\), and \(H_{c,jk}=\sum_{g\in c}u_{g,jk}\) for stratum-by-arm cell \(c\).
The support convention uses \(d_{ij}>0\) even if \(w_i=0\), matching =StratifiedCovariance=.
On the pair-supported PSUs in cell \(c\), define \(A^D_{c,jka}=\sum_{g\in c}u_{g,jk}T^D_{gja}\), \(B^m_{c,jk}=\sum_{g\in c}u_{g,jk}T^m_{gk}\), and \(P_{c,jka}=\sum_{g\in c}u_{g,jk}T^D_{gja}T^m_{gk}\).
Then
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{G_a,m}[j,k]
=\frac{1}{N_jN_k}\sum_c F_c\,\mathbf 1\{H_{c,jk}\geq2\}
  \frac{H_{c,jk}}{H_{c,jk}-1}
  \left(P_{c,jka}-\frac{A^D_{c,jka}B^m_{c,jk}}{H_{c,jk}}\right),
\qquad N_j=\sum_i d_{ij}w_i.
\]
Here \(F_c=1\) by default; with FPC it is the engine's coordinate-independent \(1-H_{sD,c}/H_s\).
A cell with fewer than two jointly supporting PSUs contributes zero; a zero \(N_j\) gives a zero row or column through the existing safe divide.

This expression is the derivative/moment cross block of the tested stratified covariance engine applied to the augmented vector \((D_{\cdot,\cdot,1},\ldots,D_{\cdot,\cdot,p},g)\).
The augmented pseudo measure uses binary copies of the original support mask and unit weights, because \(D\) and \(g\) already contain \(d_{ij}w_i\).
The engine's cached denominator is explicitly set to repeated *original weighted* \(N_j\), avoiding a second mask/weight multiplication or a switch to support counts.
This reuses pair support, within-cell centering, \(H/(H-1)\), FPC, and safe division without a parallel implementation.
It materializes the full augmented \(((p+1)M)\times((p+1)M)\) covariance to extract only the derivative/moment cross block.
The first slice is suitable for moderate \(p,M\); it does not provide a bounded-memory route.

With one observation per PSU and one cell of \(H\) fully supported PSUs, the sample expression is \(H/(H-1)\) times the centered derivative/moment cross product divided by \(N_jN_k\).
It is not bitwise equal to the uncentered IID cross product in one sample; under a zero null moment its expectation has the IID reduction.
The tested one-cell reduction therefore asserts the centered sample identity rather than an incorrect finite-sample IID equality.

* Mixed TT/SS/TS construction

For a mixed strategy, each derivative column inherits the design/sampling classification of its original moment coordinate.
Repeating the original classification over the augmented vector makes the covariance engine choose the matching TT, SS, or TS rule for every derivative/moment pair.
The all-design case delegates exactly to the pure-stratified helper.
The all-sampling case retains the selected sampling semantics.

Weighted exposure matters here.
The fixed-stratum engine projects each cell component on its original weighted exposure, not on the count of observed rows.
Its augmented pseudo measure therefore retains repeated original masks and original weights; safely dividing the adjusted contributions by their original exposure recovers pseudo residuals, with zero at zero exposure.
The cached tuple carries the actual augmented means, exposure, and original weighted denominators.
This also preserves the global centering of random-mode SS when =sampling.centered=True=, while random-mode TS remains uncentered as specified by the selected mixed covariance.
Fixed-mode SS/TS use their ordered-cell-pair projection and local trace correction instead.

The original child strategies, =couple=, =fixed_strata= and validated cell-pair geometry are retained when the moment mask is repeated.
We copy the already validated dataclass rather than rerun its eager factory under JIT.
Design FPC continues to affect TT only; random-mode sampling dof corrections continue to affect SS and TS; fixed-mode sampling dof correction remains refused by its factory.
The extracted derivative/moment cross slice need not be symmetric in its moment indices.
Like the pure-stratified helper, this construction materializes the full augmented covariance and is intended for moderate parameter and moment dimensions.

* Verification and limits

=tests/inference/test_k_statistic_stratified.py= independently loops over cells, PSUs, parameters, and moment pairs in NumPy; it covers unequal support, unequal weights, zero-weight support, FPC, \(H<2\), and zero \(N_j\).
A public K call matches the independent =score_cov_fn= oracle.
The seeded fixed-cell weak-identification null simulation (seed 141; 600 repetitions; 120 singleton PSUs, two fixed cells; \(\theta_0=0\)) produced matched mean \(p_K=0.4903\) and 30/600 rejections at nominal 5%.
Using the deliberately uncentered IID derivative/moment cross term with the same stratified \(V\) gave mean \(p_K=0.4358\) and 36/600 rejections.
This is a finite-sample regression check of the matched automatic path, not a universal calibration guarantee.
The mixed deterministic checks are in =tests/inference/test_k_statistic_mixed.py=.
They compare original-coordinate NumPy PSU/cluster and exposure-projection calculations with automatic cross covariance, including unequal and fractional masks, zero weights, FPC, centering, dof corrections, uncoupled blocks, reductions, and JIT/vmap execution.
The reproducible mixed weak-null study is =scripts/validation/mixed_k_calibration.py=; its JSON summaries report finite and invalid denominators separately for each matched or deliberately unmatched cross covariance.
No general mixed calibration guarantee follows from these deterministic identities or one study fixture.
