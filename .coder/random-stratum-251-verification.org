* Statistical target and limits

This is unconditional iid-stratum refit variability, requiring finite moments, no dominant stratum, positive limiting coordinate support and a regular consistent estimator.
Means remain pooled weighted ratios; no equal weighting of stratum means is introduced.
Rebuilding a matching mixed covariance does not prove those assumptions or equate its conditional design TT block with unconditional variation.
The exact common-profile counterexample is included as a regression: two balanced strata with profiles =(-1,+1)= yield raw mixed covariance =[[0,.5],[.5,.5]]=, while enumerated whole-stratum mean covariance is =[[.5,.5],[.5,.5]]=.
This existing #145 discrepancy is retained and documented, including the absence of a claim to repair analytic covariance, J chi-square calibration or general studentization.

FPC remains rejected because without-replacement behavior requires the population/assignment experiment.
Finer, coarser and crossing sampling partitions are rejected pending their resampling targets.
Only covariance metadata IDs are rewritten; arbitrary grouping IDs inside model-data columns require an appropriate equivariant grouping/population contract.
Weighted/sparse/nonlinear coverage beyond the deterministic oracles is not established here.
