* Exact fixed-effect identity on the frozen panel

Let \(F\) contain the product and quarter-by-market indicators, \(M_F=I-P_F\), \(Z\) the 43 frozen instruments, \(y_\theta\) the transformed outcome and \(q_\theta\) the remaining modeled terms.
The frozen columns of \(Z\) are orthogonal to both indicator sets: the largest absolute product and pair group means are \(3.41\times10^{-15}\) and \(1.03\times10^{-14}\).
These values are recorded in the frozen =native180-data-prep/results/job-40446236-report.json= under =preprojected_block_means.instruments= (input SHA256 =9939853c1691d1af6ddd05bbf505b3a7b0d8aea8a3c6974c61cdc09b9cec4307=).
Thus \(Z'M_F=Z'\) up to floating-point error, and the implemented projected moment obeys
\[
m(\theta)=N^{-1}Z'M_F(y_\theta-q_\theta)=N^{-1}Z'(y_\theta-q_\theta).
\]
Since \(F\) and \(Z\) are frozen, differentiating gives the same identity for the parameter Jacobian.
The source still differentiates through the full joint projection; this algebra is a check, not a request to replace its implementation.

The cancellation does not extend to pair covariance contributions.
Writing \(a_p(\theta)\) for fitted product effects and \(b_c(\theta)\) for pair effects, the projected total for pair \(c\) is
\[
T_c(\theta)=\sum_{i\in c}Z_i(y_{\theta i}-q_{\theta i})-\sum_p a_p(\theta)\sum_{i\in(c,p)}Z_i.
\]
The \(b_c\) term vanishes because \(\sum_{i\in c}Z_i=0\); its derivative vanishes for the same reason.
The 45 product effects are shared across pairs and remain in \(T_c\) and its derivative.
At the frozen warm point, projected and unprojected moment means agree within \(5.13\times10^{-16}\), but their pair-cluster covariance matrices differ by 32.3 percent in relative Frobenius norm.
The implementation correctly retains projected row residuals for covariance assembly.

Conditional on exact centering \(Z'F=0\) in an asymptotic sequence, the fixed-effect calculation yields a useful reduction.
For each pair, define \(B_{cp}=\sum_{i\in(c,p)}Z_i\), and let \(T_c^0\) use the population product projection coefficient \(a_p^0(\theta)\) in place of its estimated counterpart.
If the number of product effects stays fixed, the product-effect Schur matrix has eigenvalues of order \(N\), the pair sizes and required moments are uniformly controlled, and \(\widehat a-a^0=O_p(N^{-1/2})\), then \(T_c-T_c^0=-B_c(\widehat a-a^0)\).
Under a corresponding bound on the average \(\|B_c\|\|T_c^0\|\), the difference between the estimated and population-product cluster covariance sums is lower order than their \(O_p(N)\) leading term.
The same argument must hold for \(\widehat a'(\theta)-a^{0\prime}(\theta)\) to cover the Jacobian--moment cross covariance.
These are conditional algebra and rate requirements, not claims established by the frozen panel's sample Schur rank.

* Conditions a native reference still needs

The restricted nuisance has 20 identified directions: nine from the \(5\times2\) factor after its one gauge, one from \(c_0\), and ten from \(\pi\).
The interest parameter \(s\) has one direction; the claimed subvector reference is \(K\sim\chi^2_1\), with 43 moment coordinates.
For that reference, a native proof must specify a triangular-array panel design and show a joint CLT for pair totals of the projected moments and their Jacobian, a matching covariance LLN, and consistency of the derivative--moment cross covariance used by the package K statistic.
It must account for estimation of the fixed 45 product effects and their derivatives; exact cancellation of pair effects makes the 5,460 pair coefficients irrelevant to pair totals under the frozen instrument orthogonality.
It must justify independence or sufficiently weak cross-pair dependence.
The observed 5,460 pairs are 84 quarters by 65 recurring market codes, so common quarter shocks and repeated-market dependence are substantive possibilities.
A read-only warm-point two-way covariance sensitivity was roughly nine times the pair-only trace after grand-mean centering; it is exploratory because the point is nonstationary and neither dependence model has been validated.

The theorem also needs a stable finite-interior restricted branch, a population lower bound on nuisance quotient information, and optimization error that vanishes at the required stochastic rate.
The package's fixed \(\lambda^2\leq0.001\) screen is a numerical admission rule, not that rate condition.
A sample stationary point with nonnegative true horizontal curvature would establish only local sample evidence; it would not exclude a lower branch or the \(c_0\to\infty\) amplitude-zero boundary.
Ridge regularization must be asymptotically nonbinding or explicitly accounted for.
The original warm anchor and all seven saved endpoints use zero ridge, so a possible general anchored-fit/direct-K mismatch is not the cause of their failed FOCs.
The existing MCAR note explicitly does not prove joint clustered and missing-data asymptotics.
