* Non-obvious decisions

- CRN provenance :: A master key alone cannot identify the DGP; preserve an explicit shared token and exact ordered global rep indices.
- Shard completeness :: Without an expected range, concatenation can only prove contiguous inferred coverage, not detect an absent terminal shard.
- Nested models :: Sharing draws does not require sharing parameter names or J degrees of freedom; a paired J difference is descriptive, not automatically a calibrated nested-model test.
- Functional records :: Marginal SEs cannot reconstruct a functional SE; evaluate the functional and the existing joint-covariance delta method at fit time.
- Numerical rank :: Spectral truncation is an opt-in numerical policy; zero derivative at a rank-loss boundary is a separate nonregular-inference problem.
- Quotient identification :: Full observability and gauge invariance do not imply that the moment Jacobian is injective on the horizontal space.
