* Priorities and exact reproductions

Invalid grouping can silently understate variance: IDs [0,1,2] with values [1,3,5] give V=3.888889 when n_clusters=3 but V=1.111111 when n_clusters=2, without an error.
Malformed design nesting can give V=-1.25 under FPC.
These require input guards, rather than silently clipping IDs or variance.

TR false convergence and LM false saddle advisories are a coherent first solver fix; see worktree ledger Section20.
Group validation, anchored ridge context, persistence and parameter naming/binding are separate change sets so their contracts remain reviewable.
A negative traced penalty requires a named invalid-input behavior or checked error; it must not be silently clipped.

Support at zero weight follows the existing ledger Section15: observability is mask-defined, while weights define exposure and contribution.
Changing that convention would change both moment covariance and derivative/moment covariance and needs a separate statistical decision.
The old July exact-boolean predicate requirement is intentional; the old three-flags count is obsolete.
Labels for an arbitrary functional cannot safely be inferred from its Python callable.
