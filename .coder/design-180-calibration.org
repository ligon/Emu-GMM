* Definitions and invariants

The true parameter is 1 and must be an exact point of both coarse and refined grids.
A draw with nonfinite p at that point has unknown membership, not a rejection.
The fixed-curve path has no nuisance minimization and hence no profile nonconvergence event.
Capacity at the singleton truth equals coverage.
Compare grids at common scanned points: a finer grid cannot change their mathematical membership, although grid-empty events may change.
Report requested, decidable, invalid and nonconverged counts, the rejection fraction, Monte Carlo standard error and a predeclared four-MCSE falsification band.
The check tests this known DGP only; it does not establish continuous-domain capacity or profiled weak-ID coverage.
