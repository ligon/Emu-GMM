* Result and scope

The frozen panel numerically supports full column rank of the implemented projected invariant-coordinate moment design at the selected null, but it does not establish the population or cluster assumptions needed for native subvector inference.
An all-strong, finite-interior triangular-array theorem below supplies a sufficient route to \(K\Rightarrow\chi^2_1\) when both quarter and recurring-market dependence are represented by the package's two-way covariance.
The prior pair-only theorem is retained as a contrast, not as the scientific target.
The actual 84-quarter-by-65-market panel does not identify a stochastic sequence, and a counterexample shows that exact fixed-effect orthogonality cannot by itself validate the pair-only covariance.
At the saved finite trust-region point, both amplitude and interest directions have very small weighted partial information in physical coordinates, and the fit fails the mandatory first-order screen.
The seven original grid verdicts therefore remain unknown.
This note implements ledger Section 35 and extends [[file:native180-proof-probe.org][the bounded proof probe]].

* Exact moment and identification algebra

Fix \(s\), write \(\delta=\exp(-c_0)>0\), \(\Gamma=AA'\), and let \(Z\) contain the 43 frozen instruments.
The panel's instrument columns are orthogonal to the product and quarter-by-market indicators to at most \(3.41\times10^{-15}\) and \(1.03\times10^{-14}\) in group means.
For the exact-centering idealization \(Z'F=0\), the joint projection \(M_F\) satisfies \(Z'M_F=Z'\).
The package's native moment vector is consequently
\begin{equation}
m_N(s,\delta,\Gamma,\pi)=\delta b_{s,N}-\ell_N-B_N\operatorname{vec}(\Gamma)-P_N\pi,
\end{equation}
where \(b_{s,N}=N^{-1}Z'(e^{-sz}Y)\), \(\ell_N=N^{-1}Z'\log p\), \(B_N=N^{-1}Z'X_\Gamma\), and \(P_N=N^{-1}Z'X_\pi\).
The instrument/FE roundoff does not alter the recorded package-Jacobian parity below.
Without idealized exact centering, the same affine formula holds with \(b_{s,N}^{\mathrm{proj}}=N^{-1}Z'M_F(e^{-sz}Y)\); the remaining \(\ell_N,B_N,P_N\) blocks enter after the outcome projection in the implemented model.
The frozen auditor calculates this projected amplitude column directly from the saved Schur projector.
This identity is about the *aggregate moment*; the package correctly retains projected row residuals for pair covariance and derivative--moment cross covariance.
See =scripts/validation/native_projected_set_180.py:117-156= and =src/emu_gmm/measures/empirical.py:620=.

Let \(T_A(\Delta A)=\operatorname{vec}(A\Delta A'+\Delta A A')\).
For rank-two \(A\in\mathbb{R}^{5\times2}\), this tangent map has a one-dimensional rotation kernel and nine identified directions.
On any fixed smooth quotient chart, the nuisance Jacobian is
\begin{equation}
G_{\eta,N}=\left[-B_NT_A,\ -\delta b_{s,N},\ -P_N\right],
\qquad \eta=(A,c_0,\pi),\quad \dim(\eta/O(2))=20.
\end{equation}
The interest column is \(G_{s,N}=-\delta N^{-1}Z'(z e^{-sz}Y)\).
For a positive-definite population cluster covariance \(\Omega\), define \(C_A=[B T_A,P]\) on the 19-dimensional factor-plus-\(\pi\) quotient.
Pointwise local nuisance rank 20 holds exactly when \(A\) has rank two, \(C_A\) has rank 19, \(b_s\notin\operatorname{col}(C_A)\), and \(\delta>0\).
The physical amplitude's information after removing the other nuisance directions is
\begin{equation}
I_{c_0\mid A,\pi}=\delta^2\left\|(I-\Pi_{\Omega^{-1/2}C_A})\Omega^{-1/2}b_s\right\|^2.
\end{equation}
Thus a fixed positive \(\delta\) can give pointwise rank without a useful lower information bound, and no uniform physical-\(c_0\) bound is possible over \(c_0\to\infty\) when \(b_s\) and \(\Omega^{-1}\) remain bounded.
The interest column also vanishes at \(\delta=0\), where \(s\) disappears from the moment model.
This is an exact algebraic obstruction to uniform identification on an unbounded amplitude domain, not evidence of an attained boundary optimum.

At fixed \(s\), if the population \(43\times26\) map \([b_s,B|_{\operatorname{Sym}_5},P]\) has its smallest singular value bounded away from zero, then its aggregate moment is injective in \((\delta,\Gamma,\pi)\).
On the rank-two PSD subset this identifies the invariant \(\Gamma\) and \(A\) up to its expected \(O(2)\) gauge, conditional on a zero moment existing.
That rank statement alone does not make the parameter-dependent CU criterion coercive or ensure a finite sample minimum.

* Target: a two-way array and matched K statistic

Index rows by product \(p\), quarter \(t\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), and recurring market \(h\in\{1,\ldots,H\}\).
Let \(T_{th}\) be the 43-vector total of projected row contributions in intersection cell \((t,h)\), \(U_t=\sum_hT_{th}\), and \(W_h=\sum_tT_{th}\).
The package's =SumCovariance.two_way_cluster= constructs
\begin{equation}
V_{2w}=N^{-2}\left(\sum_tU_tU_t'+\sum_hW_hW_h'-\sum_{t,h}T_{th}T_{th}'\right).
\end{equation}
Its derivative--moment analogue is the identical signed sum of the three cluster-total cross products, each divided by the package's \(N_jN_k\); the automatic K path now uses that expression for uncentered clustered summands.
The subtraction removes the intersection component counted in both margins, and can produce an indefinite sample matrix that requires the package's regularizer.
The frozen panel has \(T=84\), \(H=65\), and \(T H=5460\); the theorem concerns a sequence with *both* margins increasing, not replication of that one panel.

Let \(r_{T,H}=(T^{-1}+H^{-1})^{-1/2}\), with \(T/H\) bounded above and away from zero and bounded product count per cell.
This is the natural scale when nondegenerate quarter and market components both contribute; an equivalent rate can be substituted if the declared stochastic design gives a different one.
The following assumptions are explicit proof obligations, not consequences of the FE construction or numerical rank audit.

A primitive sufficient example makes the two-way law less opaque.
Suppose \(N/(TH)\to\mu\in(0,\infty)\), \(T/H\to\rho\in(0,\infty)\), and the *population-product-effect* cell total at the null has the additive decomposition \(T_{th}^0=a_t+b_h+e_{th}\).
Let \(a_t\), \(b_h\), and \(e_{th}\) be three mutually independent, mean-zero arrays, independent within their own index sets, with uniform \(4+\epsilon\) moments, covariance limits \(\Sigma_a,\Sigma_b,\Sigma_e\), and the corresponding Lindeberg laws.
Then the row and column sums and ordinary LLNs give
\begin{equation}
r_{T,H}m^0\Rightarrow N(0,\Omega),\qquad
r_{T,H}^2V_{2w}^0\to_p\Omega
=\mu^{-2}\left[\frac{1}{1+\rho}\Sigma_a+\frac{\rho}{1+\rho}\Sigma_b\right].
\end{equation}
Indeed, \(U_t^0=H a_t+\sum_hb_h+\sum_he_{th}\), so its scaled sum of outer products tends to \(\mu^{-2}(1+\rho)^{-1}\Sigma_a\); the market term tends to \(\mu^{-2}\rho(1+\rho)^{-1}\Sigma_b\), and the intersection term is \(O_p((T+H)^{-1})\) after \(r_{T,H}^2\) scaling.
The CLT follows by applying independent-array CLTs to \(T^{-1}\sum_ta_t\) and \(H^{-1}\sum_hb_h\); the cell remainder is negligible at this rate.
The same calculation gives the signed derivative--moment cross-covariance limit when the centered derivative cell totals jointly admit such row, column, and cell components with the moment totals; the deterministic mean of a derivative cell total contributes only lower-order sample terms.
This example permits correlation across all markets in a quarter and across all quarters of a market, but it is a modeling assumption, not a representation derived from the frozen panel.
It also does not establish that estimating the product effects preserves the limits; that reduction is stated separately below.

- Two-way sampling law :: At a correctly specified finite-interior truth, \(r_{T,H}m_{T,H}(s_0,\eta_0)\Rightarrow N(0,\Omega)\) for \(\Omega\succ0\), and \(r_{T,H}^2V_{2w}(s_0,\eta_0)\to_p\Omega\).
  The quarter, market, and intersection total laws must justify the *signed* covariance convergence, including any nonzero cluster means and nonseparable interactions.
  A row/column shock decomposition with suitably independent components, Lindeberg bounds, and negligible interaction remainder is one possible primitive route, but is not verified here.
- Projected effects and derivatives :: Product-effect estimates and their derivatives have a locally uniform limit, and replacing them by their population counterparts changes \(r_{T,H}m\), \(r_{T,H}^2V_{2w}\), and the scaled signed derivative--moment cross covariance by \(o_p(1)\).
  Exact instrument orthogonality removes the product effects from the aggregate sample moment; it does not prove the two covariance reductions because the quarter and market totals still contain estimated effects.
- Local quotient identification :: On a smooth rank-two factor quotient chart, \(G_{T,H}\to_p G\), the 20-column nuisance Jacobian has full rank, and its information \(G_\eta'\Omega^{-1}G_\eta\) has a positive minimum eigenvalue.
  For the all-strong conclusion below, the residualized interest column is also nonzero, so the full quotient rank is 21.
  The restricted population branch is finite, interior, locally unique, and separated; local stochastic differentiability and uniform laws hold there.
- Weighting and curvature :: The regularized covariance satisfies \(r_{T,H}^2(V^\star-V_{2w})\to_p0\) with the local derivative control required by CU, and the package's signed derivative--moment covariance has its matched \(r_{T,H}^{-2}\) limit.
  Uniformly on shrinking neighborhoods containing the consistent restricted fit, \(r_{T,H}^{-2}\nabla_{\eta\eta}^2(Q/2)\to_p G_\eta'\Omega^{-1}G_\eta\) and \(r_{T,H}^{-2}H_\eta\to_p G_\eta'\Omega^{-1}G_\eta\).
  A sample with zero ridge does not prove the sequence requirement.
- Restricted optimization :: The computed restricted nuisance remains on that consistent branch and its half-criterion score obeys \(g_{\eta,T,H}/r_{T,H}=o_p(1)\), equivalently under the curvature assumptions to a squared Newton decrement \(g_\eta'H_\eta^{+}g_\eta=o_p(1)\).
  The fixed admission ceiling 0.001 is a finite-sample screen, not this vanishing-rate guarantee.

Under these assumptions, the package K statistic at the restricted fit, using =SumCovariance.two_way_cluster= for *both* the moment covariance and automatic derivative--moment correction, obeys \(K_{T,H}(s_0,\widetilde\eta)\Rightarrow\chi^2_1\).
/Proof./ Let \(Z_0\sim N(0,I_{43})\), \(R=I-\Pi_{\Omega^{-1/2}G_\eta}\), and whiten by the limiting two-way \(\Omega\).
The joint CLT, covariance law, local score equation, and nonsingular nuisance information give \(r_{T,H}\Omega^{-1/2}m(s_0,\widetilde\eta)=RZ_0+o_p(1)\).
The matched correction in \(\widetilde D=G-\widehat\Sigma_{G,m}(V^\star)^{-1}m\) is \(O_p(r_{T,H}^{-1})\) under the assumed joint derivative laws, so its whitened column space converges to that of \(\Omega^{-1/2}G\).
The package projects the whitened moment onto this column space.
Nuisance projection has already removed 20 directions; the independent interest direction contributes a rank-one projection of \(RZ_0\), whose squared norm is \(\chi^2_1\). \(\square\)

If the interest column is weak, the preceding full-rank-interest assumption must be *replaced*, not retained: require \(R_{T,H}\to_pR\) and joint convergence of the nuisance-residualized whitened moment and properly scaled corrected interest column to \((RZ_0,d)\), where \(d\in\operatorname{range}(R)\), \(\Pr(\|d\|>0)=1\), and \(d\) is independent of \(RZ_0\).
Then the limiting rank-one K projection is \((d'RZ_0)^2/(d'd)\sim\chi^2_1\).
This process-level corrected-derivative condition has not been established for the native two-way projected panel.

* Pair-only theorem as a contrast

Let \(C\to\infty\) index quarter-by-market pairs, with \(N_C/C\to\mu\in(0,\infty)\), fixed 45 products, 43 moments and a rank-two factor.
Keep the package convention \(m_C=N_C^{-1}\sum_cT_c\) and \(V_C=N_C^{-2}\sum_cT_cT_c'\); define \(r_C=N_C/\sqrt C\) only for this proof.
There is no new \(N\) multiplier in the implemented criterion.
The following assumptions are sufficient, not properties verified by the frozen panel.

- Projection and product effects :: Along the array, \(Z_C'F_C=0\) exactly or with error negligible after \(r_C\) normalization; the product contrast Schur matrix divided by \(C\) has eigenvalues uniformly bounded above and away from zero.
  The 45 product-effect coefficients and their parameter derivatives have deterministic population targets and locally uniform \(O_p(C^{-1/2})\) errors.
  Pair sizes, instrument totals, residual totals and derivatives have bounded moments sufficient for the reductions below.
- Pair limit :: At a correctly specified finite-interior truth \((s_0,\eta_0)\), let \(T_c^0\) and \(D_c^0\) use the population product coefficients.
  The centered moment sum obeys \(C^{-1/2}\sum_c T_c^0\Rightarrow N(0,\Omega)\), with \(\Omega\succ0\), and \(C^{-1}\sum_cT_c^0T_c^{0\prime}\to_p\Omega\).
  Matching derivative--moment products converge to their actual joint covariance limits.
  For the package's uncentered pair products, pair-specific means must vanish or have a separately justified negligible contribution.
  Independent mean-zero pairs with appropriate Lindeberg and moment bounds suffice; merely allowing unspecified cross-pair dependence does not.
- Local smoothness and rank :: Uniform laws and stochastic differentiability hold near the true point on a fixed smooth factor quotient chart, giving \(G_C\to G\) of full column rank 21, including the interest direction.
  The factor stays rank two, \(\delta_0>0\), and the restricted population branch is locally unique and separated.
  Uniformly on a neighborhood containing the consistent restricted fit, \(r_C^{-2}\nabla_{\eta\eta}^2(Q_C/2)\to_p G_\eta'\Omega^{-1}G_\eta\) and \(r_C^{-2}H_{\eta,C}\to_p G_\eta'\Omega^{-1}G_\eta\), with the latter positive definite on the 20-dimensional nuisance quotient.
- Weighting :: The CU estimator and K evaluator use asymptotically equivalent ingredients: \(r_C^2V_C\to_p\Omega\), and their matched derivative--moment covariance estimates remain at the required \(r_C^{-2}\) scale.
  Any anchored ridge or override obeys \(r_C^2(V_C^\star-V_C)\to_p0\), with local derivative control.
  Zero ridge in this single panel does not imply that sequence condition.
- Restricted fit :: At \(s=s_0\), the computed nuisance stays on the consistent finite-interior branch and its half-criterion score satisfies \(g_{\eta,C}/r_C=o_p(1)\).
  Under the preceding local curvature conditions this is equivalent to a squared Newton decrement \(g_{\eta,C}'H_{\eta,C}^{+}g_{\eta,C}=o_p(1)\).
  A fixed admission ceiling of 0.001 does not imply this rate.

Under these conditions, the full-vector package K value evaluated at the restricted fit and referenced to the one interest direction satisfies \(K_C(s_0,\widetilde\eta_C)\Rightarrow\chi^2_1\).

/Proof./ Write \(Z_0\sim N(0,I_{43})\), \(\Gamma_\eta=\Omega^{-1/2}G_\eta\) and \(R=I-\Pi_{\Gamma_\eta}\).
The pair CLT, covariance law, full-rank nuisance expansion and \(o_p(1)\) scaled score yield \(r_C\Omega^{-1/2}m_C(s_0,\widetilde\eta_C)=RZ_0+o_p(1)\).
The package's derivative--moment correction is \(O_p(r_C^{-1})\) under the stated scales, so its corrected, whitened Jacobian has the same limiting column space as \(\Omega^{-1/2}G\).
The K value is the squared length of the whitened moment projected onto that full column space (=src/emu_gmm/inference/k_statistic.py:627-692=).
Since \(R\) removes exactly 20 nuisance directions and the full column space adds one independent interest direction, the surviving projection of \(RZ_0\) has rank one and standard-normal coefficient.
Its squared length is \(\chi^2_1\). \(\square\)

For the product-effect reduction, put \(B_{cp}=\sum_{i\in(c,p)}Z_i\).
The exact pair identity in the prior proof note is \(T_c-T_c^0=-B_c(\widehat a-a^0)\); the aggregate identity \(\sum_cB_c=0\) follows from product centering.
If \(C^{-1}\sum_c\|B_c\|^2=O(1)\), \(C^{-1}\sum_c\|T_c^0\|^2=O_p(1)\), and \(\widehat a-a^0=O_p(C^{-1/2})\), Cauchy--Schwarz makes the difference between fitted and population-product covariance sums \(o_p(1)\) after division by \(C\).
The same calculation with \(D_c\) and \(\widehat a'\) covers the derivative--moment products.
This is conditional on the rate and moment bounds; connectedness and sample Schur rank do not prove them.

The theorem deliberately assumes all 21 quotient directions strongly identified.
If the interest column is local-to-zero, its random corrected derivative can determine the K projection.
A sufficient *replacement* for the full-rank interest assumption, retaining strong nuisance identification and the restricted-fit expansion, requires the nuisance projection \(R_C\to_p R\) and joint convergence of the nuisance-residualized whitened moment and appropriately scaled corrected interest column to \((RZ_0,d)\), where \(d\in\operatorname{range}(R)\), \(\Pr(\|d\|>0)=1\), and \(d\) is independent of \(RZ_0\); then the limiting K value is \((d'RZ_0)^2/(d'd)\sim\chi^2_1\).
Deriving that condition at the *estimated* nuisance requires a process-level limit for the corrected derivative and mixed parameter derivatives, not just a joint CLT at the true point.
The original [[https://www.princeton.edu/~erp/GNYC/papers/kleibergen.pdf][Kleibergen--Mavroeidis subvector paper]] gives a \(\chi^2\) upper-bound argument under its CU assumptions; it does not supply the missing native projected-panel array conditions.

* Why fixed-effect orthogonality does not validate pair-only clustering

Here is a concrete counterexample using the same 65 recurring markets and fixed product count.
For markets \(m=1,\ldots,65\), let \(b_m=1\) for 32, \(-1\) for 32 and 0 for one; choose product loadings \(a_p\) with \(\sum_pa_p=0\) and \(A_2=\sum_pa_p^2>0\).
Use a balanced one-row-per-product-and-pair array.
For each quarter \(t\), let \(U_t\) be independent, mean zero and unit variance, and set an instrument \(z_{ptm}=a_pb_m\) and residual \(u_{ptm}=a_pb_mU_t\).
Both \(z\) and \(u\) have zero product and quarter-by-market means, so joint FE projection changes neither of them.
Nevertheless, the pair total is \(T_{tm}=A_2 b_m^2U_t\).
The variance of the sum over one quarter is \(A_2^2(\sum_m b_m^2)^2=4096A_2^2\), whereas the expected pair-only outer-product sum is \(A_2^2\sum_m b_m^4=64A_2^2\).
As the number of quarters grows, the pair sandwich understates the actual variance by a factor of 64, despite exact FE orthogonality and a valid quarter-level CLT.
For this construction, the market and intersection terms have equal expected sums, so the two-way inclusion--exclusion expression recovers the quarter component in expectation.
This proves that the observed centering checks cannot establish the native pair covariance limit; it does *not* assert that the frozen data contain these shocks.
Repeated-market shocks can create an analogous omitted cross-pair term over time.
The frozen consumer's =tmp/cereal_gmm_estimate.py:47-63= defines \((t,m)\) pair IDs to handle correlation *within* a pair; it does not state independence across recurring markets or common quarters.

* Frozen-panel identification audit

The independent NumPy auditor =scripts/validation/native_identification_180.py= checks the pinned data, warm direction and exact replay postfit-array hashes before using the 43 instrument columns and saved package weighting matrix.
It compares the analytic restricted Jacobian with the package result to maximum absolute gap \(9.77\times10^{-15}\), and \(G'\Lambda G\) with the saved GN matrix to \(1.08\times10^{-12}\).
The all-symmetric \(43\times26\) *implemented projected* linear moment design for \((\delta,\Gamma,\pi)\) has numerical rank 26; its smallest raw singular value is 0.00818046 and its column-normalized minimum is 0.0166183.
Its projected amplitude column differs from the unprojected idealization by Euclidean norm \(1.996\times10^{-15}\); Weyl's bound leaves the projected minimum singular value above 0.00818045994.
Thus this frozen sample has no numerical aggregate-moment alias in those invariant coordinates at fixed \(s\), at the stated SVD threshold.
That is stronger than local rank on the rank-two submanifold, but is neither a population rank lower bound nor evidence of a CU optimizer minimum.

At the saved RTR point \(\delta=0.00083761021895\), the other 19 identified nuisance directions have smallest positive weighted singular value 0.07058659.

* What is established and what remains

The exact algebra gives a necessary-and-sufficient *pointwise* nuisance rank condition, and the frozen sample passes an even stronger invariant-coordinate rank check.
The sufficient theorem states the two-way projected-panel and finite-interior conditions that would make a *two-way* \(\chi^2_1\) reference valid; the package now computes its matched K correction for uncentered clustered signed sums.
The pair-only theorem remains conditional, and the counterexample proves that FE orthogonality and pair count cannot validate it for the declared quarter-and-market target.
No two-way stochastic CLT, consistent signed covariance law, population singular-value bound, asymptotically negligible ridge, or stationary finite-interior restricted branch has been demonstrated for this native panel.
The next proof obligation is a primitive quarter-and-market array model that establishes the moment, derivative and fitted-product-effect laws, followed by a branch/boundary analysis of the restricted CU criterion before any saved point is called an optimum.
Issue #180 and the original seven unknown verdicts remain open.
