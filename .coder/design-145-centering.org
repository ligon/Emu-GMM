* Opt-in implementation slice (2026-10-23)

=DesignAwareCovariance.from_design_mask(..., fixed_strata=True)= selects a
conditional-on-fixed-strata sampling covariance.
The default remains =fixed_strata=False=: the original random-stratum
uncentered =ClusteredCovariance= sampling block and cross pass are unchanged.
Use the opt-in when the nuisance profile is a fixed conditional mean of
observed moment contributions within each stratum-by-arm cell, sampling clusters are
nested in strata, and multiple clusters support the moment pairs of interest.
In the opt-in mode, the design block \(V_{TT}\) remains the original
=StratifiedCovariance= result, while one pair-supported exposure-projection
kernel supplies both \(V_{SS}\) and \(V_{TS}\).
This retains the #109 coupled/uncoupled decomposition.

For each ordered pair of cells \((c,d)\) in one stratum and moments \((j,k)\),
the implementation uses the projected numerator
\((M_{c,j}t_{c,j})'(M_{d,k}t_{d,k})\) on the clusters supporting both cell components.
Here \(t_{h,c,j}=\sum_{i\in h,c}d_{ij}w_i\psi_j(x_i,\theta)\),
\(e_{h,c,j}=\sum_{i\in h,c}d_{ij}w_i\), and
\(M_{c,j}=I-e_{c,j}(e_{c,j}'e_{c,j})^{-1}e_{c,j}'\) on that pair-supported set.
Each term receives its own \(H_{cd,jk}/\operatorname{tr}(M_{c,j}M_{d,k})\)
factor when \(H_{cd,jk}\geq2\) and both exposure norms and the trace are positive.
This factor is justified by homogeneous cluster cross covariance within each
cell pair; it is a working correction under heterogeneity.
The sum over ordered \((c,d)\) is the expansion of the covariance of the
sum of cell components: for a variance, the \((c,d)\) and \((d,c)\)
cross terms are both required, and are not duplicate estimates.
If every sampling cluster belongs to one cell, cross-cell terms have zero
support, reproducing the design engine's within-cell Neyman assembly.
If a sampling cluster pairs distinct arms, the cross-cell terms retain its
shared shock while removing fixed arm-specific cell means.
The covariance kernel stores dense \(G\times C\times M\) cell components
and \(P\times M\times M\) pair geometry, where
\(P=\sum_s C_s^2\) counts /within-stratum/ ordered cell pairs.
It does not construct all cross-stratum \(C^2\) pairs during covariance
evaluation.
The detailed diagnostic expands to \(C\times C\times M\times M\) only
when requested, so its memory cost should be considered for many cells.
For \(H=2\), nearly orthogonal exposure vectors make the trace tiny and
the raw correction unstable.
The implementation treats a cell pair as unsupported when
\(\operatorname{tr}(M_{c,j}M_{d,k})\leq\sqrt{\epsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}}H_{cd,jk}\), where the
machine epsilon matches the cluster-total dtype.
The cutoff is a numerical guard; its effect on coverage requires separate
study.
The local factor corrects for estimating the cell mean under the
homogeneous-cluster working model.
The constructor rejects =sampling.dof_correction=True= in this mixed opt-in mode
because the global \(G_{jk}/(G_{jk}-1)\) would add a second inflation.
The all-design reduction remains exactly =StratifiedCovariance= and does not
use the sampling correction, regardless of this flag.
The default random-stratum mode retains the #119 correction unchanged.
Scaling remains \(1/(N_jN_k)\).

A cell pair with \(H_{cd,jk}<2\), zero exposure norm, or trace below
the numerical guard contributes exactly zero.
The \(H<2\) part matches the design-side unsupported-pair rule.
=fixed_cell_pair_diagnostics(measure)= returns \(C\times C\times M\times M\)
arrays for each ordered cell pair's support count, projection trace, and
exact usability flag.
=fixed_stratum_pair_diagnostics(measure)= returns \(S\times M\times M\)
arrays summarizing distinct cluster count, the /sum/ of usable cell-pair traces,
and whether /any/ cell-pair term contributes.
Its summed trace is not one projection trace; inspect the cell-pair diagnostic
to locate which term is guarded out.
=fixed_stratum_pair_support(measure)= remains a count-only convenience.
Callers can inspect losses from both low support and the trace guard before
inference.
The constructor refuses sampling clusters crossing strata and a design with
at most one sampling cluster in every observed stratum.
It requires finite, exactly integer-valued cluster, stratum, and cell IDs
in this opt-in mode before casting them; otherwise rounding could silently
change their nesting relation.
One sampling cluster per stratum cannot identify a random stratum shock
separately from an arbitrary fixed profile; the opt-in does not estimate
that unconditional random-stratum component.
The current default is the path for that target.
No general finite-sample guarantee is claimed for heteroskedastic clusters,
stochastic observability, or ratios with random \(N_j\).
The deterministic tests in
=tests/covariance/test_design_aware_fixed_strata.py= establish algebra and
compatibility, not coverage; the separate Monte Carlo evidence below is
specific to the stated fixed-profile and paired-shock designs.

* Recommendation and limit

For a fixed /per-observation/ cell profile, project each cluster's cell
component on its observed weighted exposure within each cell-pair overlap set.
This has a well-defined target when the caller's sampling clusters are nested in strata and at least two clusters support a pair in an informative stratum.
It cannot identify a random cross-arm stratum shock when the stratum itself is the sole sampling cluster: subtracting that stratum's estimated mean removes the shock, while leaving it uncentered confounds the shock with an arbitrary fixed stratum mean.
That case needs either repeated cross-arm sampling clusters within strata or
an externally specified mean model; the mixed opt-in rejects the all-singleton
configuration eagerly.

The implementation slice above is opt-in; the pre-revision numerical checks
below test its local correction under a paired-cluster working model.
The distinction between fixed and random stratum effects and the
heterogeneous-cluster finite-sample correction remain substantive limits.

* Superseded bare-stratum candidate algebra

This section records the first implementation's rationale for audit.
The 300-rep falsification above supersedes its bare-stratum projection;
the revised kernel projects separate cell components and sums ordered
cell-pair terms.

Let \(h\) index the supplied sampling clusters and \(s(h)\) their fixed stratum, with each \(h\) contained in exactly one stratum.
For coordinate \(j\), define \(t_{h,j}=\sum_{i\in h}d_{ij}w_i\psi_j(x_i,\theta)\), \(q_{h,j}=\mathbf 1\{\sum_{i\in h}d_{ij}>0\}\), and \(N_j=\sum_i d_{ij}w_i\).
For each pair \((j,k)\), set \(u_{h,jk}=q_{h,j}q_{h,k}\) and, within stratum \(s\),
\[
H_{s,jk}=\sum_{h:s(h)=s}u_{h,jk},\quad
A_{s,jk}=\sum_{h:s(h)=s}u_{h,jk}t_{h,j},\quad
B_{s,jk}=\sum_{h:s(h)=s}u_{h,jk}t_{h,k},\quad
P_{s,jk}=\sum_{h:s(h)=s}u_{h,jk}t_{h,j}t_{h,k}.
\]
For equal exposure per overlapping sampling cluster, the centered numerator is \(C_{s,jk}=P_{s,jk}-A_{s,jk}B_{s,jk}/H_{s,jk}\) for \(H_{s,jk}\geq2\), and zero otherwise.
In that balanced special case, a common sampling kernel gives both the \(SS\) and \(TS\) entries by block mask:
\[
V^{\mathrm{ctr}}_{jk}=\frac{1}{N_jN_k}\sum_{s:H_{s,jk}\geq2}
\frac{H_{s,jk}}{H_{s,jk}-1} C_{s,jk}.
\]
The \(TT\) block remains the existing cell-centered =StratifiedCovariance= output, so the all-design path is untouched.
For either centering rule, the \(TS\) corners must come from the /same/ sampling kernel as \(SS\), masked by \(P_{TS}\); =cross_block= and =couple=False= then retain the #109 identity by construction.
The design FPC remains confined to \(TT\).

Pair-specific overlap is essential: a per-coordinate stratum mean uses different cluster sets in the two factors and can create a spurious off-diagonal under missingness.
The weights enter \(t_{h,j}\) once; \(N_jN_k\) remains the only sample-size normalization.
With a fixed /cluster-total/ shift \(t_{h,j}=a_{s,j}+\epsilon_{h,j}\) on pair-supported clusters, \(C_{s,jk}\) is invariant to \(a_{s,j}\) and \(a_{s,k}\).

A fixed /conditional mean of moment coordinate \(j\) among observed units/ shifts \(t_{h,j}\) by \(a_{s,j}e_{h,j}\), where \(e_{h,j}=\sum_{i\in h}d_{ij}w_i\).
The displayed cluster-total centering does not remove that shift when \(e_{h,j}\) varies.
An exposure-adjusted alternative uses a pair-specific projection onto each coordinate's exposure vector.
On the \(H_{s,jk}\) clusters where /both/ coordinates have \(q_{h,j}=q_{h,k}=1\), let \(e_j=(e_{h,j})_h\) and \(M_j=I-e_j(e_j'e_j)^{-1}e_j'\), and likewise for \(k\).
The cross numerator \(t_j'M_jM_k t_k=(M_jt_j)'(M_kt_k)\) removes any fixed shift proportional to \(e_j\) or \(e_k\), including unequal masks and weights.
For independent clusters with the same cross covariance \(\sigma_{jk}\) within a stratum, its expectation is \(\sigma_{jk}\operatorname{tr}(M_jM_k)\); the corresponding correction is \(H_{s,jk}/\operatorname{tr}(M_jM_k)\).
When exposures are constant and equal on that overlap set, this reduces to \(H_{s,jk}/(H_{s,jk}-1)\).
With heterogeneous cluster covariances the trace factor is only a working approximation; an exact finite-sample correction needs additional assumptions or a different estimator.
The chosen fixed-effect definition therefore matters under the package's unequal-support contract.
This pair-supported projection is the candidate for the package's general mask/weight regime; the simpler \(P-AB/H\) formula is its equal-exposure reduction.
For the #130 residual moments \(r z_j\), a fixed outcome profile can also multiply /random within-stratum variation/ in \(z_j\); that variation belongs in \(V_{SS}/V_{TS}\) and is not erased by projecting only their conditional moment means.
The mask is treated as fixed in this covariance calculation; if stochastic observability changes the stratum mixture in \(N_j\), a separate unconditional ratio-estimand derivation is needed.

** The #119 finite-sample choice

The displayed local Bessel factor corrects the loss of one estimated mean /per stratum and pair/ under equal cluster exposure.
The default random-stratum #119 contract makes =sampling.dof_correction=True= multiply a raw cross product by global \(G_{jk}/(G_{jk}-1)\), where \(G_{jk}=\sum_s H_{s,jk}\) (=src/emu_gmm/covariance/clustered.py:141-159=).
Those factors are not interchangeable: with \(S>1\) fixed strata and balanced support, global \(G/(G-1)\) times the centered numerator has expectation \((G-S)/(G-1)\) of the target under independent equal-variance clusters.
An ablation multiplied the local correction by the inherited global factor in
/both/ \(SS\) and \(TS\).
With 15 paired clusters, its expectation exceeds the analytic cross target by
\(15/14\); the separate paired-cluster check measured that excess.
With exposure projection, the local factor becomes \(H_{s,jk}/\operatorname{tr}(M_jM_k)\) only under equal cross covariance across overlapping clusters.
The general heterogeneous-cluster normalization remains open, so the global #119 factor must not be combined with a guessed local one.
The opt-in uses only the local factor and rejects the global flag explicitly;
the literal #119 on/off contract remains on the default mode.

* Discriminating fixtures and falsification

1. Fixed profile :: At least three strata, several sampling clusters per stratum, two arms, \(t_{h,j}=a_{s,j}+\epsilon_{h,j}\) with an unequal fixed \(a_s\) and a no-profile control.
   With equal cluster exposures, the centered \(SS/TS\) blocks must be invariant to adding \(a_s\) to every supported cluster in a stratum; the current uncentered blocks must move.
   Repeat under ragged support and unequal weights, checking \(H_{s,jk}\), \(N_jN_k\), and an independent NumPy formula; then add a fixed conditional /moment mean/ per observed unit and verify that simple cluster-total centering fails while exposure-projection centering is invariant.
2. Genuine cross-arm shock :: Within each stratum create at least two /paired/ sampling clusters, each containing distinct treatment and control PSUs.
   Draw a new pair shock \(z_{s,h}\) each replication; put \(z_{s,h}\) in a treatment-only design moment and \(\rho z_{s,h}\) in a control-only sampled moment, plus independent arm noise and a fixed \(a_s\).
   Give the paired PSUs one =sampling.cluster_id= but distinct =design.psu_id=; the true cross-arm covariance is \(\rho\operatorname{Var}(z)\neq0\), while PSU clustering sees zero cross-arm overlap.
   The pair-supported exposure projection must keep the signed nonzero term after removing \(a_s\), and a sign flip of \(\rho\) must flip it.
   A shock shared by /every/ cluster in one stratum is deliberately removed along with its fixed profile; that is the one-cluster-per-stratum identification limit.
3. Falsifiers :: Set \(\rho=0\) (cross-arm term should approach zero); use one sampling cluster per stratum (centering cannot estimate its cross-arm term and must refuse or mark it unsupported); drop pair support in selected strata (those \(H_{s,jk}<2\) terms must be exactly zero); set all moments to design (bit-for-bit old output); check \(V_{\mathrm{coupled}}=V_{\mathrm{uncoupled}}+V_{TS}\) and cache/JIT parity.

The paired-cluster fixture tests a regime that the current #130 study does not contain.
A separate MC check on its known DGP should compare empirical cross covariance, coverage, ridge binding, and convergence, with a fixed seed and CRN arms, before changing the default.

* Limits after the first slice

- The opt-in estimates a conditional-on-fixed-strata covariance, while the
  unchanged default retains the random-stratum sampling target.
  A one-cluster-per-stratum design cannot identify both an arbitrary fixed
  profile and a random stratum shock without more information.
- The centered mode requires sampling clusters nested in strata and exposes
  unsupported pairs through the count, trace, and usability diagnostic.
  Its zeroed pairs can matter for inference and should be inspected.
- The local correction is the opt-in finite-cluster convention under the
  homogeneous-cluster working model; the extra global correction is rejected.
  The covariance ablation tests its mean, while estimator coverage still
  needs the planned larger Monte Carlo comparison.
- Is the nuisance profile additive to each sampling /cluster total/ or to each observed /moment contribution/ before masking and weighting?
  The #130 DGP adds a fixed outcome profile to \(r\); its effect on each moment's mean depends on the instrument \(z_j\), so the exposure-projection correction and its heterogeneous-cluster behavior require derivation before claiming a general fix.
- Is observability conditioned on as a realized design, or part of the sampling law for \(N_j\)?
  The latter can make changes in stratum composition random even when the stratum-specific moment means are fixed.
